03 / My perspective

Beyond artificial intelligence.

What if we began to think of AI not merely as artificial intelligence, but as a form of synthetic intelligence — something we learn to relate with, understand, and work alongside?

I'm interested in sharing reflections on this shift, especially from the perspective of computer science students navigating a fast-changing world. The future need not be framed only as humans versus machines; it can also be about how we think, create, and grow together.
